Returns & Refunds

At DEVI, we strive for perfection in every stitch. If your purchase isn't exactly what you envisioned, our return process is designed for absolute clarity and ease.

Conditions for Returns

verified

Unworn & Original State

Items must be returned in their original condition, unworn, unwashed, and with all DEVI designer tags and security seals intact.

schedule

14-Day Window

Return requests must be initiated within 14 calendar days of the delivery date as confirmed by the courier service.

inventory_2

Exclusions

Custom-made Lehengas and items marked as 'Final Sale' at the time of purchase are not eligible for returns or exchanges due to their bespoke nature.

The Return Process

Step 01

Initiate Online

Log into your DEVI account, select the order in 'My Orders', and click 'Request Return' to generate your unique return ID.

Step 02

Pack Securely

Place the item back into its original silk-lined box. Include the printed return authorization slip inside the package.

Step 03

Courier Pickup

Schedule a complimentary pickup via our luxury logistics partner using the link provided in your return confirmation email.

Refund Timelines

Stage Duration
Quality Inspection 48 Hours
Processing Refund 3-5 Business Days
Bank Reconciliation 7-10 Business Days

Note: Refund timelines depend on your financial institution's internal processing speeds.
